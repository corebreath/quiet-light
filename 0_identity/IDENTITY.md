# Project Identity — The Quiet Light

> Working name: **The Quiet Light**. A children's inner-world journey app.

## What is this?

A story-driven mobile app for children ages 8–12 that teaches the single most important
thing either parent or therapist rarely says directly:

**Inside you, there is a place that is always safe.**

The app is the third in a trilogy — a curriculum layer that ties together Felt Sense
Friends (somatic body awareness) and Grounded Kids (energy + elements) into one
continuous inner journey. Each app is a world. This app is the heart of the map.

## The North Star (every design decision runs through this)

> After every session, the child feels: **"I am safe inside myself."**

Not happy. Not calm. Not fixed. Safe. As in: there is a place inside me that no storm
can reach. I know how to find it. I can always come back.

## Core Metaphor

The child has an **inner world** — a living landscape that reflects their inner state.
Weather changes with how they feel. But at the center, through all storms, one thing
never changes: a small, warm, unflickering **Quiet Light**.

That light is the real self. The part that was never hurt. The part that knows.

## Who is it for?

- Children 8–12, used independently (solo, no parent required)
- Children already using Felt Sense Friends and/or Grounded Kids
- Families with lightly spiritual framing (not religious doctrine, not pure secular)
- English-speaking initially; Spanish expansion after v1

## Source Teachings

Synthesized from two traditions (see `3_reference/`):
- **Berkeley Psychic Institute (BPI)** — grounding, energy, the witness position,
  "you are a spirit in a body," neutrality, amusement
- **Roy Masters (FHU)** — the false self / real self, the observation, stillness,
  "be still and know," inherited emotional patterns, "whose feeling is this"

## The Three Worlds (curriculum map)

| World | App | Teaching |
|---|---|---|
| The Feeling Forest | Felt Sense Friends | Feelings are visitors. You are the home. |
| The Earth Chamber | Grounded Kids | You are connected. Roots hold you. |
| **The Quiet Chamber** | **This app** | **The still point. The real you. Always safe.** |

## Non-Negotiables

- **The Quiet Light never fully goes dark.** No matter the child's state, the light
  is always visible. Safety is unconditional.
- **No streaks, no shame loops.** Practice is invited, never demanded.
- **No cognitive prompts during emotional states.** Body-first, stillness-first.
- **The Echo (false self) is gentle and confused — never threatening or scary.**
- **Local-first, COPPA-safe.** No PII leaves the device. All progress is on-device.
- **Guided voice narration for every practice.** No reading required mid-session.
- **Solo use by design.** The child navigates independently, no adult required.

## What this is NOT

- Not a therapy app. Not marketed as treatment.
- Not a gamified streak machine.
- Not religious doctrine — lightly spiritual, non-denominational.
- Not dependent on the other two apps (standalone, but enriched by the trilogy).
- Not a replacement for connection with real humans.
