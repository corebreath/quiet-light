## Chapter Texts (Journey Tab)

### ch1 — The Light Inside
1. Once, before anyone taught you anything, before you learned how to be afraid or angry or sad — there was a light inside you.
2. It was quiet. It was warm. And it belonged only to you.
3. People may have tried to change it. Life may have covered it with clouds. But the light never went out. Not even once.
4. That light is what you really are. Not the clouds. Not the storms. The quiet, steady light underneath.
5. In this journey, you will learn to find it again. And once you find it, you'll always know the way back.

### ch2 — The Weather of Feelings
1. Have you ever woken up and felt like a storm was already happening inside you?
2. Or maybe you felt foggy — like you couldn't quite see or think clearly.
3. Feelings are like weather. They come, they move through you, and they go.
4. Sunny days happen. Stormy days happen. Foggy days happen too. None of them last forever.
5. You are not the storm. You are the sky that holds all kinds of weather — and keeps going.

### ch3 — The Still Pond
1. Imagine a pond deep inside you. When everything is quiet, the surface is perfectly still.
2. When a thought comes, it drops into the pond like a pebble. Ripples spread out.
3. You might feel pulled to follow the ripple — to think about the thought, to worry, to react.
4. But the pond doesn't become the ripple. You are the water. You can watch the ripples without being swept away.
5. When you stop feeding the ripples, the pond goes still again. This stillness is always available to you.

### ch4 — The Voices in the Wind
1. Not every thought in your head was made by you.
2. Some thoughts were put there by people you love. By things you heard at school. By shows you watched. By worries that don't belong to you.
3. These thoughts blow through you like voices in the wind. They're real — but they're not all yours.
4. You can learn to notice: "Is this thought something I truly believe? Or is it just passing through?"
5. You don't have to believe every thought you have. You are the one who watches thoughts — you are not the thoughts.

### ch5 — The Echo
1. Sometimes you feel something strongly — anger, or sadness, or fear — and you don't know why.
2. Here's a secret: feelings can travel. When someone near you is angry, that anger can move into you, like an echo bouncing off a wall.
3. You might feel their sadness as your own. Their worry as your worry.
4. This isn't your fault. It happens to everyone. But you can learn to notice it.
5. The question to ask is: "Is this feeling mine? Did I make it? Or did I catch it from someone else?"

### ch6 — The Shadow Friend
1. There is a part of you that sometimes acts in ways you don't like. Gets angry too fast. Says things you regret. Feels jealous or mean.
2. Some people call this the Shadow. But the Shadow isn't bad — it's just confused.
3. The Shadow learned to act this way to protect you. It was trying to help — it just doesn't know a better way yet.
4. "I see you. I know you're scared. I don't need you to protect me this way anymore." You can say this quietly, inside.
5. The Shadow gets smaller when you're kind to it. Because underneath, it just wants to be safe — just like you.

### ch7 — The Quiet Chamber
1. Inside you, there is a room that belongs only to you.
2. No one else can come in without your permission. No noise from outside can reach it unless you let it.
3. In this room, your quiet light burns steadily. Always. Even on the worst days.
4. Whenever life gets too loud, you can come here. Just close your eyes and remember: there is a room inside me. And I am safe there.
5. You will practice finding this room. And the more you visit, the easier it gets to find.

### ch8 — The Gardener
1. Imagine your inner world is a garden.
2. Some plants grew on their own — fears planted by hard times, worries that took root without your permission.
3. But you are the gardener. You get to decide what grows.
4. Every time you practice coming back to your quiet center, you are watering the light inside you. It grows a little brighter.
5. You can't fix everything at once. But you can tend your garden, a little bit, each day. That's all it takes.

## Practice Scripts

### Morning Check-in (checkin.tsx)
1. Take a slow breath. Feel where your feet meet the ground.
2. Now go inside. What's the weather like in your inner world right now?
3. Whatever it is — sunny or stormy — it's okay. It's just weather. It will change.
→ Then: tap your weather (5 options)

### Quick Quieting (short.tsx)
1. Sit comfortably. Feel your feet on the ground.
2. Take a slow breath in... and let it go.
3. Notice the quiet inside you. It's always there — like a small flame that never goes out.
4. If thoughts come, that's okay. Just notice them and let them pass. You are the one who notices.
5. Rest here. You are safe inside yourself.

### The Still Pond (pond.tsx)
1. **The Pond** — Imagine a still pond inside you. When everything is quiet, the surface is perfectly smooth. You can see all the way to the bottom.
2. **The Ripples** — Now imagine a pebble drops in. Ripples spread out. A thought. A feeling. A memory. They ripple across the pond...
3. **You are the water** — But notice — the pond doesn't become the ripple. The water watches the ripple. You are the pond. The thoughts and feelings are just ripples passing through.
4. **Watch without following** — A thought appears: let it ripple and pass. Another comes: let it go too. You don't have to follow every ripple. You can just watch.
5. **The stillness returns** — As you stop feeding the ripples, the pond grows still again. This stillness is always here, underneath everything. This is you.
6. **Practice now** — Close your eyes if you'd like. For one minute, just watch. Notice thoughts and feelings come and go. You are the watcher.

### Deep Practice (long.tsx)
1. **Come inside** — Find a comfortable position. Let your body settle. You don't need to do anything right now — just be here.
2. **Feel the ground** — Notice your feet. Notice where you're sitting. The earth is holding you. You don't have to hold yourself up right now.
3. **Three breaths** — Take three slow breaths. With each exhale, let go of anything you were holding onto. You can pick it up again later if you need it.
4. **Find the quiet** — Somewhere inside you, there is a place that is always quiet. It doesn't get louder when life gets loud. It just stays still.
5. **The light** — In that quiet place, there is a small light. It was there before any of your problems. It will be there after. It's what you really are.
6. **Notice the weather** — What's the weather like in your world right now? Rain? Sun? Storm? Just notice it — like a sky watching its own clouds.
7. **You are not the weather** — The weather is real. But you are not the storm. You are the sky. The sky holds all kinds of weather without becoming any of them.
8. **What belongs to you?** — Is there a heavy feeling you've been carrying? Quietly ask: Is this mine? Did I make this? Or did I catch it from someone around me?
9. **Return to the light** — Come back to that quiet flame inside you. Whatever the answer was — you're okay. The light is still here. It always will be.
10. **Slowly return** — When you're ready, take a breath. Feel your feet. Feel your hands. Gently come back to the room. Carry the quiet with you.

### Quiet Chamber (chamber.tsx)
*Open-ended, no steps. Full-screen sanctuary.*

> This is your safe place inside.
> Nothing from outside can reach you here.
> No one needs anything from you right now.
> You can just rest.
> Stay as long as you need.
